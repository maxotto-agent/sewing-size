How this works, and what it leaves out
  • Sizes come from each brand's published body-measurement chart, re-entered in centimetres (not the garment measurements). Each range shows its source link and the date it was checked.
  • Bust: if your full minus high bust difference is larger than the cup the range is drafted for, the bust column is matched using your high bust plus the drafted difference. That is the usual "size by high bust" advice. Ranges with no stated cup (alpha ranges, Plus) use the number named in the row.
  • Where a chart gives one number per size, "between 12 and 14" means you are more than 1 cm from both. Where it gives ranges, "gap" means your measurement falls between two sizes' ranges, and "overlap" means it falls in both.
  • Charts don't include ease. Your pattern's finished measurements and your own fit preferences still decide. Size charts and brands change; check the source before buying.
  • Brands covered so far: Closet Core, Friday Pattern Co, Style Arc, Simplicity (Misses' and Plus), Cashmerette, True Bias, Grainline Studio, Named Clothing. Friday's own page shows slightly different tables for XL and up; this tool uses its centimetre table.
